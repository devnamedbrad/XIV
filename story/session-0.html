Session 0

After reading the introduction the players should start piece by piece creating a character as you introduce bits of the 1.0 story, in preparation to send them into ARR for session 1. Expect session 0 to take between 2 and 4 hours. All dependent on your players ability to decide on characters, and how much they want to learn about events.

Important events in preferred order are detailed below.

Welcome to Eorzea

First, have your players choose their race, class and name. Having them decide their class can help guide a new player to which city would best fit their profession. While they don't have to start in that jobs usual city, it can help build an affiliation to a city early on.

For Ishgardian based jobs, it might be that the Player learned the basics of their class, but while Ishgard closed down its gates further they were unable to return. The Player then began to travel to the other city states. For reaper it's almost a given that the player will be, or at least be in contact with, Garlean defectors. Perhaps fleeing after their outlawing in Garlemald, or a forced conscript who learned the skills and escaped service. The Lemures could arrive after session 0 and be an interesting thread of connection and distrust.

The young years

Each of the Players when creating their character should work through their life's history. They can leave it to the dice in the PHB, or work with GM to create a story that makes sense. But it should be made clear that at some point in their lives, they witnessed the same event as many others - a meteor shower on the day of the Battle of Silvertear Skies. They experienced a sharp pain in their head, and passed out. Maybe this event led them to go and adventure, or perhaps to study the event at a major city.

The players don't need to know each other at the start, as they will meet later. However, certain jobs can lend to easy connections of history. A Summoner and Scholar would both start as Arcanists for Limsa Lominsa, so they may have become friends. A Paladin could easily befriend a Dancer who had been working near the quicksands, or a monk as they sparred for the gladiator ring. A White Mage and Dragoon could easily start as part of a Gridanian greenhorn unit. A Dark Knight and Astrologian could be connected to some corruption of the Holy See and flee for different reasons without knowing each other, or the Dark Knight could be protecting a falsely accused "heretic" Astrologian.

For jobs where you begin as another class, work with you Player to establish that history and start their story - a pugilist gets trained by a sparring partner who was a Monk in Ala Mhigo. A rising gladiator may be known for their honor in duels and The Sultansworn take notice. Though not all need to be connected to their previous jobs - a ninja could just be someone from the East who traveled to Eorzea to escape Garlean rule.

Players can also add to their story by apprenticing at a guild or learning another trade in their years. These can add a little flavor or connection to their city. Some examples are below

Once they have established their home, you can take players by their starting city and introduce them to Minfilia, the Path of the Twelve, and the Circle of Knowing.

The path of the Twelve

Each of the Players will have a hint of the echos actual gift, ignited by the vision of a meteor shower seen again. They will be introduced to Minfilia via the people in their visions, and prepare for the path to Dalamud's descent. Depending on their history, you can adjust the following, but for each players starting city, read the following and allow for perception checks, or encourage them as you see fit.

Gridania

The Order of the Twin Adder asks that all new recruits take small missions to assist the God's Quiver and Wood Wailers in the hunt for oddities and poachers. Despite seeing a very out of place masked Hyur and staff wielding Lalafell, your trip was uneventful. After a week in the woods, your group begins its trek back to Gridania. As you pass the Ampitheater, you begin to notice a small gathering of people and a Padjal trying to calm them.

"Please, the greenwrath can be cleansed. Krim and all of you will be ok!" The Padjal yells. You notice in the crowd the Lalafell and Hyur you saw days ago. In the center of this group is a young boy, frozen in place. The Padjal begins chanting quietly, and you could swear you see a small winged creature follow his staff. He touches his staff to the head of the red haired child, and he begins to move again. As he does, the sky above turns into a dark black swirling storm. Meteors begin to fly forth from it, and you head stings, knocking you out. When you awaken, you are outside of Gridania, being helped by a hermit. "You. I have a feeling you saw something important."

He gives you water to drink, and asks if this is the first time this has happened. "I see. I can't help you with it, but I know others who might. Ul'dah. Merchants ward." He scribbles a name onto the paper and leaves the waterskin with you. "Best to leave before the Twelveswood swallows you too." The man calmly walks into the forest, and quickly disappears.

NOTE - Have your player roll a DC 8 perception check to notice that this man has a similar hair color, and the same angry expression as the frozen child, Krim. On a 12 they conclude it must be a sibling of them. They are in fact Krim, the frozen child in the vision.

Limsa Lominsa

The Maelstrom has assigned you and other recruits to investigate a potential pirate fugitive - one who has attacked Limsa's own ships. You make you way through the coast of Eastern La Noscea, your party takes a break as you keep watch and explore. Around a corner, you see a Miqote in white, and she looks to be protecting a Hyur pirate with a green mask against a Roegadyn clad in military red.

As you run toward them, the pirate in red is struck by a large black orb, and falls limp to the ground. His chest glows white as a horn is magically lifted out of his pocket, and flies fast to a well dressed Elezen who disappears into a cloud of black mist. As he does, the clouds in the sky turn a dark black, and a storm of meteors begins to appear. Your head stings, your body goes limp, and as you awaken you see a man with glasses checking on you. Your Maelstrom friends have started to run over and the man tells you to meet him at the Drowned Wench, urgently.

Hours later you meet the man, who calls himself Emerik. He tells you that he thinks he knows what caused your pain, hands you a note, a sack of gil for a boat ride, and tells you to visit a shop in the Merchants area of Ul'dah. "I will see you soon, friend." He chugs the last of his ale and walks off, coin left for the bill.

NOTE - the Player can roll a DC 8 perception check to notice that this man has a similar jaw and hair color to the masked pirate. On a 12 they conclude it must be a sibling of them. They are in fact Emerik, the masked pirate in the vision.

Ul'dah

After assisting the Immortal Flames in a fight with the Amaljaa, you and your crew return to Ul'dah. You pass by a young white haired man with a girl on each arm, and a dagger attached to his belt. As you pass a corner in the streets near the Hall of Flames, you almost bump into a young girl and her father. "Look! Fireworks!" shouts the girl. You hear the distinct sound of the fireworks as the father yells "Asillia, slow down!" You notice the street has gotten a lot busier. A celebration? A parade platform with a Goobue chained up passes by you. As you turn to look at the sky for the fireworks, what was once a clear day has darkened and a swirling vortex forms. From its center a soft red glow, then meteors.

You feel a sting of pain in your head and, fall to the ground. As you awaken, you see the girl from before crying. Next to her is the Goobue and many others, dead. Its body is covered in cuts, burns and several daggers. Beneath part of it, the remains of what looks like her father. You blink once, and it all fades. There is nothing in that area. No parade. No Goobue. But next to you is a blonde woman, "would you mind coming with me?" she asks as she offers you a hand up.

She takes you to the Merchants ward, into a seemingly beat down building nestled in the corner. "There will be some others visiting today, if my information is correct. Please, sit here and Tataru will bring you a drink."

NOTE - the Player can roll a DC 8 perception check to notice that this woman looks incredibly similar to the child in the accident. On a 12 they conclude it must be a sibling of them. They are in fact Minfilia, the child in the vision.
